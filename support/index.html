The “Ask AI” button or generated quiz questions don't work

With Apple Intelligence selected, these features need a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in the Settings app. Curated content, review and the built-in quizzes work on every device.

How do I use OpenAI instead?

Go to Settings → AI Provider in the app, choose OpenAI, and paste your own API key from your OpenAI account. Usage is billed by OpenAI to your account. See the privacy policy for what is sent to OpenAI.

How do I remove my OpenAI API key?

Go to Settings in the app and tap Clear next to the key. Deleting the app also removes it.

How do I get new content?

The app checks for updated study content automatically when it launches with an internet connection. Everything you've already downloaded keeps working offline.

How do I reset my review progress?

Review progress is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.
