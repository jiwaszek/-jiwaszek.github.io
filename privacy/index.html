Privacy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

I build small, independent apps. They are designed to keep your data on your device. This page describes the principles all of my apps follow. Each app also has its own privacy policy that explains exactly what that app does with data. If an app's policy is more specific than this page, the app's policy applies.

App privacy policies

  • CSDeveloper

Principles

  • No accounts. My apps don't ask you to sign up or log in.
  • No tracking or advertising. My apps don't track you across apps or websites, don't show ads, and don't include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Your data stays on your device unless an app's policy says otherwise and explains why.
  • No selling of data. I don't sell, rent or share your personal data for marketing.
  • Third-party services are disclosed. If an app sends data to a third party, such as an AI provider, the app's policy names that service and lists what is sent.

Crash reports and diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide me with anonymized crash reports and usage statistics. This is controlled by Apple and you can turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

My apps are not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If these principles or an app's data practices change, I'll update the relevant page and its “Last updated” date.
